Classical, to start with.

New to classical music? This is the program I’d hand you.

  1. I

    “O mio babbino caro”

    Puccini

    From Gianni Schicchi. Three minutes long, and the aria that makes most people fall for opera.

    Listen
  2. II

    “Habanera”

    Bizet

    From Carmen. You already know it, even if you don’t know its name.

    Listen
  3. III

    Clair de lune

    Debussy

    For quiet evenings and slow Sunday mornings.

    Listen
  4. IV

    Piano Concerto No. 2

    Rachmaninoff

    Sweeping and romantic. Hear it live if you ever get the chance.

    Listen
  5. V

    Symphony No. 1 in E minor

    Florence Price

    The first symphony by a Black woman performed by a major American orchestra, in 1933.

    Listen
  6. VI

    Symphony No. 9, “From the New World”

    Dvořák

    Start with the second movement, the Largo.

    Listen

Galleries in LA.

The places I send friends when they visit.

  • The BroadDowntownFree general admission. Reserve the Infinity Mirror Rooms ahead.→
  • MOCA Grand AvenueDowntownAcross the street from The Broad, so make a day of both.→
  • LACMAMiracle MileStay until dusk, when Urban Light comes on.→
  • The Getty CenterBrentwoodThe gardens and the view are worth the trip on their own.→
  • Hammer MuseumWestwoodFree, thoughtful and a few minutes from UCLA.→
  • California African American MuseumExposition ParkFree, and always showing something worth a second visit.→
  • Hauser & WirthArts DistrictA gallery with a courtyard garden. Stay for lunch.→
