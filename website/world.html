My content.

I make short videos that explain opera, ballet, theater and art history to people who think the arts aren’t for them. I connect what’s on stage to the films, songs and moments people already know, so a 150-year-old opera feels as current as anything on their feed.

What I make
Short-form video on Instagram: I talk to camera, add the history, and bring in the clips, paintings and posters that tell the story.
Who it’s for
Curious people who are new to the arts, and arts lovers who want the backstory.
What I cover
Opera and musical theater, ballet, art history, film, and the tech and culture around them.

For brands

Work with me on content.

I partner with arts organizations, fashion and beauty brands and tech companies that want to reach people who are curious about culture. I handle the idea, the script, filming and editing, and I can host on camera at your event.

Classical, to start with.

New to classical music? This is the program I’d hand you.

  1. I

    “O mio babbino caro”

    Puccini

    From Gianni Schicchi. Three minutes long, and the aria that makes most people fall for opera.

    Spotify
  2. II

    “Habanera”

    Bizet

    From Carmen. You already know it, even if you don’t know its name.

    Spotify
  3. III

    Clair de lune

    Debussy

    For quiet evenings and slow Sunday mornings.

    Spotify
  4. IV

    Piano Concerto No. 2

    Rachmaninoff

    Sweeping and romantic. Hear it live if you ever get the chance.

    Spotify
  5. V

    Symphony No. 1 in E minor

    Florence Price

    The first symphony by a Black woman performed by a major American orchestra, in 1933.

    Spotify
  6. VI

    Symphony No. 9, “From the New World”

    Dvořák

    Start with the second movement, the Largo.

    Spotify
