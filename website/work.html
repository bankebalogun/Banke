For teams and hosts

Opera & ballet 101.

Lots of people would like to go to the opera or the ballet but don’t know where to start. I teach the basics in a relaxed, fun way: the story, the music, what to listen and look for, and how a night at the theater works. I serve on the Board of Directors of the LA Opera League and create arts content with Segerstrom Center for the Arts and Broadway.

  1. I

    Corporate events

    A lunch-and-learn, an offsite or a client event. An intro to opera, ballet or classical music your team will actually enjoy.

  2. II

    Hosted dinners

    A private or client dinner where I talk through the opera, the ballet or an exhibition between courses.

  3. III

    Before the show

    Taking a group to a performance? I’ll brief everyone on the story and what to watch for before the curtain goes up.

  4. IV

    Virtual sessions

    The same 101, online, for remote and hybrid teams.
