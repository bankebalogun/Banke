About Banke

Healthcare,humanity, impact.

Healthcare Solutions Consultant · Speaker · Mentor · Model · Philanthropist

Los Angeles · Available worldwide

Banke Balogun is a Health Technology Solutions Consultant, speaker and thought leader working at the intersection of healthcare, artificial intelligence, innovation and leadership. She helps healthcare organizations modernize how they work by translating complex technology into practical business solutions, while also helping professionals navigate careers in an industry being reshaped by AI and digital transformation.

She spent more than a decade in healthcare management, leading multisite physician practices and hospital operations for major health systems, including Dignity Health, before transitioning into healthcare technology. Today she partners with healthcare organizations to modernize operations, improve digital workflows and advance more efficient, patient-centered care.

Banke earned her Master of Public Health from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), where she developed a strong foundation in healthcare systems, population health and innovation. After building her career through a nontraditional path from public health into enterprise technology, she has become a recognized voice on the future of healthcare, emerging technologies, career mobility and personal branding. Her perspective is grounded in both public health and enterprise technology, allowing her to bridge conversations between healthcare, business and innovation.

Alongside her work in health technology, Banke is an international speaker who has presented for conferences, universities, organizations and technology communities across the United States and internationally. Whether speaking to executives, students, founders or emerging professionals, she is known for making complex topics accessible while inspiring audiences to think bigger about the future of healthcare, innovation and their own careers.

Banke’s work extends beyond the stage through her commitment to philanthropy, civic leadership and the arts. She serves on the Board of Directors of the LA Opera League, where she helps advance arts education, community engagement and support for one of the nation’s leading opera companies. She believes innovation is not limited to technology, and that investing in culture, education and community is equally important in shaping stronger societies.

Beyond her professional career, Banke is passionate about mentorship, leadership and creating opportunities for others. She is the former co-founder of Naija Gal Network, the first international organization dedicated to connecting Nigerian women through professional networking, mentorship and cultural celebration.

As a public voice, Banke is passionate about expanding access to opportunities for women and professionals entering technology from nontraditional backgrounds. She frequently shares insights on health technology, artificial intelligence, leadership, career growth and the future of work, with the goal of helping others build meaningful and impactful careers.

Her work has been featured in Vogue and Essence, and she has appeared on podcasts, panels and industry stages discussing the future of healthcare, innovation and technology.

“Technology is most powerful when it creates better outcomes for people.”
