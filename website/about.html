Hi, I’m Banke.

Healthcare solutions consultant, speaker, mentor, model, philanthropist and community leader in Los Angeles.

'26 3 14

My work sits where healthcare, technology, culture and impact meet.

I spent more than a decade in healthcare management, leading multisite physician practices and hospital operations for major health systems, including Dignity Health. Then I moved into healthcare technology. Today I partner with healthcare organizations to modernize operations, improve digital workflows and make care more patient-centered.

I earned my Master of Public Health at UCLA, where I built a foundation in health systems, population health and innovation. That mix of public health and enterprise technology is what I bring into every room.

I speak at conferences, universities and tech communities in the US and abroad, from Black Is Tech in Houston to Black Tech Fest in London. I like making complicated topics simple, and pushing people to think bigger about healthcare and about their own careers.

Mentorship matters a lot to me. I co-founded Naija Gal Network, the first international organization connecting Nigerian women through professional networking, mentorship and culture.

Outside of work, I serve on the Board of Directors of the LA Opera League. I’m a former board member of Jazz Empowers, a former member of the Junior League of Los Angeles and a member of the Beverly Hills Chamber of Commerce. My work has been featured in Vogue and Essence.

Technology is most powerful when it creates better outcomes for people.
