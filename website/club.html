The Intermission Club.

For everyone who lights up at an overture, lingers in galleries and knows the best seat in the jazz club. We go to the shows together, and the best conversations happen at intermission.

Join the club →

Art is better shared. No one should skip the show because they’re waiting on a friend to say yes. Come solo, leave with a crew.

Banke Balogun, founder
What we do

Nights out, together.

Opera nights

Group seats at LA Opera, with drinks before the show and a debrief after.

Broadway & theater

Touring Broadway shows and local premieres, from Costa Mesa to Downtown LA.

Jazz evenings

Live sets at the city’s best listening rooms, with good company.

Galleries & museums

Openings, art fairs and slow weekend visits, followed by brunch.

See you at intermission.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is it for?

Everyone who loves the arts, or wants to. First-timers and season-ticket holders alike.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Joining is free, and everyone buys their own tickets, often at a group rate.

Do I need to know a lot about the arts?

Not at all. Curiosity is plenty.

Where do we go?

Across Los Angeles and Orange County, from Downtown LA to Costa Mesa.
