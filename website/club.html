The IntermissionClub.

For everyone who lights up at an overture, lingers in galleries and knows the best seat in the jazz club.

Join the club
Why it exists

Art is better shared. No one should skip the opera, the play or the show because they’re waiting on a friend to say yes. Come solo, leave with a crew.

Gatherings

What we do together.

Opera nights

Group seats at LA Opera, with a pre-show toast and post-show debrief.

Concert evenings

Symphonies and recitals in the city’s great halls, then dinner nearby.

Gallery walks

Openings, art fairs and studio visits, with a spotlight on Black and African artists.

Salon afternoons

Recitals and readings in historic rooms, with good company.

Museum mornings

Unhurried weekend visits, followed by brunch.

After the show

Where the best conversations happen: a nightcap and a debrief.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is it for?

Everyone who loves the arts, or wants to. All backgrounds, first-timers and season-ticket holders alike.

Is it free to join?

Joining is free. Members cover their own tickets, often at a group rate.

Do I need to know a lot about the arts?

Not at all. Curiosity is the only requirement.

Where do gatherings happen?

Across Los Angeles and Orange County, from Downtown LA to Costa Mesa.

Correspondence

For speaking, advisory, partnerships and press, write to bankebalogun@gmail.com. Based in Los Angeles, available worldwide.
