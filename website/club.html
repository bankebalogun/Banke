The Encore Society.

An arts community for Los Angeles and Orange County. We go to the opera, theater, concerts, jazz and galleries together, so the arts become something you share.

Join the society →

Los Angeles has world-class stages all over the city. What a lot of us are missing is someone to go with.

Banke Balogun, founder
Why it exists

The gap we’re closing.

LA is full of people who love the arts, or would if they gave them a try. Plenty of them never go. It’s rarely a lack of interest. It’s a few quiet barriers.

  1. 01

    No one to go with

    Friends are busy, plans fall through, and going alone feels like a lot. So the tickets never get bought.

  2. 02

    Not sure where to start

    Opera, the symphony, a gallery opening. It can feel like there’s a dress code and a rulebook nobody shared.

  3. 03

    It feels like it’s for someone else

    A lot of arts audiences don’t look like the city around them. When you don’t see yourself in the room, it’s easy to stay home.

What we do about it

Nights out, together.

Each month we pick a few events, sort out group seats and go as a group. You get a familiar face at the door, a little context before the curtain goes up, and good conversation after.

Opera nights

Group seats at LA Opera, with drinks before the show and a debrief after.

Broadway & theater

Touring Broadway shows and local premieres, from Costa Mesa to Downtown LA.

Jazz evenings

Live sets at the city’s best listening rooms, with good company.

Galleries & museums

Openings, art fairs and slow weekend visits, followed by brunch.

The value of it

Why it matters.

Real connection
A shared evening is one of the easiest ways to make friends as an adult, in a city where that isn’t always easy.
Fuller theaters
When more of us show up, LA’s theaters, orchestras and galleries can keep doing ambitious work.
Something for you
A few hours away from your phone, watching people at the top of their craft. You leave a little different from how you came in.
Room for everyone
Men and women, every background and every age, first-timers and season-ticket holders. You don’t need to know anything to belong.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is it for?

Anyone in LA or Orange County who loves the arts or is curious about them. Men and women, any age, first-timers and season-ticket holders.

Can I come alone?

That’s the point. Most people come on their own the first time and leave knowing a few faces.

Do I need to know a lot about the arts?

Not at all. We share a little background before each show, and curiosity is plenty.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Joining is free, and everyone buys their own tickets, often at a group rate.

Where do we go?

Across Los Angeles and Orange County, from Downtown LA to Costa Mesa.
