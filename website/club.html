An arts community for Southern California

The Encore Society

For people in LA who love the ballet, museums, theater and music and want people to go with. Joining is free.

How it worksJoin the society →

0105

The problem

Why I started it.

I kept hearing the same thing from people in Los Angeles. They wanted to see the ballet, a new exhibition, a Broadway show or a lecture, but didn’t have anyone to go with. As I met more people, I realized there’s a whole community here who are into this. They just hadn’t found each other.

  1. 01

    No one to go with

    Friends are busy or not into the same things, plans fall through, and the tickets never get bought.

  2. 02

    Going alone

    So people end up at the ballet, the museum or a show by themselves, or they skip it.

  3. 03

    Hard to find each other

    There are plenty of people in LA who love this stuff. There just hasn’t been an easy way to meet them.

What it is

The Encore Society is a group chat for people in Southern California who love the arts. Every month I share a list of things worth seeing. Members pick what they want to go to and make plans with each other, so no one has to go alone.

Cost
Free to join. You buy your own tickets.
Where
A WhatsApp group, for events across Southern California.
Who
Anyone who loves the arts or is curious about them, from first-timers to season-ticket holders.
How it works

One list a month.

At the start of each month I post a list of events for the weeks ahead. Everything else happens between members.

  1. I

    Join the group

    Request to join below and I’ll add you to the WhatsApp group.

  2. II

    Get the list

    At the start of each month I post a list of ballet, museums, Broadway and off-Broadway, concerts and lectures around LA.

  3. III

    Make plans

    Say what you’re going to, find others who want to go, and buy your own tickets. Members organize themselves.

On the list

What you’ll find.

Opera, ballet, Broadway and off-Broadway, the symphony, jazz, museums and lecture series, from the major houses to smaller rooms worth knowing.

Opera & ballet

Classic repertoire, new work and the ballet.

Broadway & off-Broadway

Touring Broadway shows, smaller stages and local theater.

Symphony & jazz

Orchestras, summer concerts and jazz clubs.

Museums & lectures

Exhibitions, art fairs, gallery openings and lecture series.

The value of it

Why it matters.

Real connection
Going to a show with people who love it too is one of the easiest ways to make friends as an adult, in a place as spread out as LA.
Fuller theaters
When more of us show up, Southern California’s theaters, orchestras, museums and galleries can keep doing ambitious work.
Room for everyone
Every background and every age, first-timers and season-ticket holders. You don’t need to know anything to belong.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is it for?

Anyone in Southern California who loves the arts or is curious about them, whatever your age or background.

Is there a cost?

Joining is free. You buy your own tickets for whatever you decide to go to.

Do you organize the outings?

I put together the list. Members decide what they want to go to and make plans with each other in the group.

How often is the list shared?

Once a month, at the start of the month. I’ll share the odd extra event in between when something good comes up.

Where does it happen?

In a WhatsApp group. The events are all over Southern California: Downtown LA, Hollywood, Pasadena, Orange County and beyond.

Do I need to know a lot about the arts?

No. Curiosity is enough.

I run an arts organization. Can we be involved?

Yes. See above or email bankebalogun@gmail.com.
