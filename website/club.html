The Encore Society.

An arts society for Southern California, free to join. I share a curated list of performances and exhibitions, and coordinate everyone who would like to attend.

Join the list →

'26 11 04

Southern California has world-class stages everywhere you look. What a lot of us are missing is someone to go with.

Banke Balogun, founder
How it works

A curated calendar.

The Encore Society is free. I don’t charge for anything I organize.

  1. I

    The list

    I put together a list of upcoming performances, exhibitions and events across Southern California and share it with everyone on the list.

  2. II

    Your choice

    You tell me which ones you’d like to attend. Come to one, a few, or all of them.

  3. III

    The coordination

    I coordinate everyone who’s going, so you arrive as part of a group instead of on your own.

Why it exists

Why I started it.

Southern California is full of people who love the arts, or would if they gave them a try. Plenty of them rarely go, and it usually isn’t for lack of interest.

  1. 01

    No one to go with

    Friends are busy, plans fall through, and going alone feels like a lot. So the tickets never get bought.

  2. 02

    Not sure where to start

    Opera, the symphony, a gallery opening. It can feel like there’s a dress code and a rulebook nobody shared.

  3. 03

    It feels like it’s for someone else

    A lot of arts audiences don’t look like the city around them. When you don’t see yourself in the room, it’s easy to stay home.

The value of it

Why it matters.

Real connection
A shared evening is one of the easiest ways to make friends as an adult, in a place as spread out as Southern California.
Fuller theaters
When more of us show up, Southern California’s theaters, orchestras and galleries can keep doing ambitious work.
Room for everyone
Men and women, every background and every age, first-timers and season-ticket holders. You don’t need to know anything to belong.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is it for?

Anyone in Southern California who loves the arts or is curious about them. Men and women, every background and every age, first-timers and lifelong fans.

Do I need to know a lot about the arts?

No. Plenty of people on the list are going to their first opera or ballet.

Is there a cost?

No. The Encore Society is free, and I don’t charge for the events I coordinate.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Most people do, and you’ll be going with the rest of the group.

Where are the events?

All over Southern California: Downtown LA, Hollywood, Pasadena, Orange County and beyond.

How do I hear about upcoming events?

Join the list above and I’ll send you the next list of events.
