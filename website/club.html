A community for the arts in Southern California

The Encore Society

A free community for people in Southern California who love the ballet, theater, music and museums, and want to meet others who do too.

Why I started itJoin the community →

0105

LA has some of the best stages in the world. They’re even better with company.

Banke Balogun, founder
What I’m solving

Why I started it.

I kept hearing the same thing from people in Los Angeles. They wanted to see the ballet, a new exhibition, a Broadway show or a lecture, but didn’t have anyone to go with, so they went alone or skipped it.

As I met more people, I noticed something else. A lot of them had no idea there was a whole community in Southern California who love this stuff too. They just hadn’t found each other. So I started one.

Before
  1. You see a ballet you’d love to go to.
  2. You text a few friends. No one can make it.
  3. You go alone, or you don’t go at all.
With The Encore Society
  1. Someone shares it in the group.
  2. You say you’re going, and a few others are too.
  3. You meet at the theater and talk about it after.
Our mission

The Encore Society brings together people in Southern California who love the arts, so finding someone to go with is easy and nobody has to miss out.

  1. I

    Connect

    Meet people in Southern California who love the same things you do.

  2. II

    Share

    When something good is coming up, it gets posted in the group, by me or by other members.

  3. III

    Go together

    Say you’re going, see who else is, and make a night of it. Everyone buys their own tickets.

Cost
Free to join. You buy your own tickets.
Where
A WhatsApp group, for events across Southern California.
Who
Anyone who loves the arts or is curious about them, from first-timers to season-ticket holders.
Who it’s for

Is this you?

If any of these sound familiar, I started this for you.

  • You’re new to LA
  • Your friends aren’t into the ballet
  • You’ve always wanted to try the opera
  • You usually go on your own
  • You have season tickets and want company
  • You love museums and lectures
  • You didn’t know there were others like you

How it works

Simple on purpose.

There’s no set schedule. Events get shared whenever something good is happening, and you go to whatever you like.

  3. III

    Go together

Get involved

Ways to be part of it.

The Encore Society is built by the people in it. Come to one thing or help bring people together.

  1. I

    Join and go

    Fill out the questionnaire, join the group and come along to whatever catches your eye.

  2. II

    Share what’s on

    Seen a show, an opening or a talk coming up? Post it in the group so others can join.

  3. III

    Host an outing

    Pick an event and get people together before or after, for a drink, dinner or a walk through the museum.

  4. IV

    Bring your organization

    Run a theater, company, museum or lecture series? Partner with the society.

How we do things

The community.

Everyone’s welcome
First-timers, lifelong fans and everyone in between. Every age and background.
Come as you are
You don’t need to know anything about the arts to belong here. Curiosity is enough.
Look out for each other
Say hello to new people, include them in plans and keep the chat kind.
No fees, no pressure
Joining is free. Go to as much or as little as you like, and buy your own tickets.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is it for?

Anyone in Southern California who loves the arts or is curious about them, whatever your age or background.

Is there a cost?

Joining is free. You buy your own tickets for whatever you decide to go to.

How often do events come up?

Whenever something good is happening. There’s no set schedule, and you only go to what interests you.

Who plans the outings?

Everyone. I share events, and so do members. Anyone can say they’re going, and others join in.

What happens after I fill out the questionnaire?

I read every one. Then I’ll reach out and add you to the WhatsApp group.

Where does it happen?

In a WhatsApp group. The events are all over Southern California: Downtown LA, Hollywood, Pasadena, Orange County and beyond.

Do I need to know a lot about the arts?

No. Curiosity is enough.

I run an arts organization. Can we be involved?

Yes. See above or email bankebalogun@gmail.com.
