About Banke

Banke is a Solutions Consultant in Health Tech and a speaker on the intersection of technology, healthcare and innovation. After breaking into tech from a non-traditional background, she now speaks on health tech, AI, personal branding and career growth.

She holds a Master of Public Health from UCLA, serves on the Board of the Opera League of Los Angeles, and is a member of the Junior League of Los Angeles.
