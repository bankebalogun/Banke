Read the full bio

Banke Balogun is a healthcare solutions consultant, speaker, mentor, model, philanthropist and community leader whose work bridges healthcare, technology, culture and impact.

She spent more than a decade in healthcare management, leading multisite physician practices and hospital operations for major health systems, including Dignity Health, before moving into healthcare technology. Today she partners with healthcare organizations to modernize operations, improve digital workflows and advance more efficient, patient-centered care.

Banke earned her Master of Public Health from UCLA. An international speaker, she has presented at conferences, universities and technology communities in the US and abroad, from Black Is Tech in Houston to Black Tech Fest in London.

She is the former co-founder of Naija Gal Network, the first international organization connecting Nigerian women through professional networking, mentorship and culture, and the founder of The Encore Society, a free arts community for Southern California. She also creates arts content with Segerstrom Center for the Arts and Broadway. She serves on the Board of Directors of the LA Opera League, is a former board member of Jazz Empowers, a former member of the Junior League of Los Angeles and a member of the Beverly Hills Chamber of Commerce. Her work has been featured in Vogue Germany, Essence and Allure.
