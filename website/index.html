How they connect

All three

People are sometimes surprised that I do all three. Healthcare is my career. Modeling was my first job, and it taught me how to walk into a room. The arts are what I do with my evenings. Each one has changed how I do the others.

Content creator

Opera, ballet and the arts.

I make content about opera, ballet and classical music for people who are new to them, and I create arts content with Segerstrom Center for the Arts and Broadway. Some of it is the history behind a piece, some is the tech backstage, and a fair amount is what to wear.

Community

The Encore Society.

A lot of people in LA love the arts but don’t have anyone to go with, and many don’t know there’s a whole community here who feel the same. So I started one. Members share events as they come up and make plans to go together. Joining is free.
