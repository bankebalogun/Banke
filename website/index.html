My story

How I got here.

I earned my Master of Public Health at UCLA and spent more than ten years in healthcare management, running multisite physician practices and hospital operations for health systems including Dignity Health. Then I moved into healthcare technology. Today I help healthcare organizations modernize how they work, speak at conferences in the US and abroad, and spend as many evenings as I can at the opera.
